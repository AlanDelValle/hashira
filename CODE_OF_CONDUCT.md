# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## Our commitment

Everyone who takes part in Hashira — contributors, reviewers, issue reporters and
maintainers — should be able to do so without harassment, regardless of experience level,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neurotype, appearance, body
size, ethnicity, nationality, age, religion or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with reasoning. "This will break when the polygon is
  self-intersecting" is useful; "this is bad code" is not.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that other people have different constraints, priorities and amounts of time.
- Assume the other person is trying to help, especially across a language barrier.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, fix them and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments — including ones dressed up as jokes or
  as technical bluntness.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions to wear people
  down.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and
code comments — and anywhere someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer at the contact address on the GitHub profile
of [@AlanDelValle](https://github.com/AlanDelValle). Reports are handled confidentially. You
will get an acknowledgement, and you will not be asked to justify why the behaviour bothered
you.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will decide on a response proportionate to what happened. Depending on
severity and pattern, that can mean a private word, a public correction, editing or removing
the offending content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who break these
rules are held to them at least as strictly as anyone else.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is written for this project and draws on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org). It is intentionally short: a
document nobody reads protects nobody.
